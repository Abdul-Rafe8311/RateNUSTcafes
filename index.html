About the App

Built for real NUST students.

Concordia Eats is a verified student review platform for the cafes on NUST campus. Sign in with your NUST institutional email — @seecs.edu.pk, @nbs.nust.edu.pk, @smme.nust.edu.pk and more — then start rating dishes, vibes, and overall experience.

All reviews are completely anonymous. No politics, no bias, no agenda — just honest opinions from the people who eat there every single day.

Simple Process

How it Works

Three steps and you're contributing to the most honest food review platform on NUST campus.

01
Login with NUST

Use your NUST institutional email (@seecs.edu.pk, @nbs.nust.edu.pk, @smme.nust.edu.pk, @scme.nust.edu.pk, @s3h.nust.edu.pk & others). No outsiders. Only verified NUST students get in.

02
Pick a Concordia Cafe

Browse every cafe on campus. See what others are saying, which dishes are trending, and who's winning the weekly leaderboard.

03
Rate & Review Anonymously

Rate individual dishes, leave a comment, and submit — completely anonymously. Your identity stays private. Your verdict stays public.

Platform Features

Everything you need.

Built to make campus food reviews actually useful — not just a star and a vague one-liner.

⭐

Dish-Level Ratings

Don't just rate the cafe — rate the biryani, the chai, the paratha. Granular ratings mean you actually know what to order before you walk in.

Anonymous Comments

Say exactly what you think without worrying who's reading. All comments are 100% anonymous — no names, no handles, no traces.

🏆

Weekly Leaderboard

See which Concordia cafe is winning this week. Rankings reset every Monday based on fresh student ratings — no stale scores, no old glory.

NUST-Only Access

Only verified NUST institutional emails (@seecs.edu.pk, @nbs.nust.edu.pk & all other departments) can post reviews. Completely free from outsider noise.
